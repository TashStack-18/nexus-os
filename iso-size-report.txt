3. ARCHITECTURAL FEASIBILITY ANALYSIS: CAN A LINUX DESKTOP BE < 100 MB OR < 500 MB?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Can the complete Debian XFCE desktop be under 100 MB?
  - NO. The compressed Linux kernel (`vmlinuz`) and `initrd.img` alone require 70 MB.
  - Adding the essential C library (`libc6`), systemd, Xorg, GTK3, XFCE desktop, and
    Python runtimes requires at least 400 MB compressed.
  - Therefore, achieving < 100 MB for a complete Debian live OS without gutting the
    desktop environment, GTK applications, and system utilities is physically impossible.

* Can the complete Debian XFCE desktop be under 500 MB?
  - Only if Snapd/Snap Store (1.2 GB uncompressed), Firefox (295 MB), and compiler tools
    are entirely uninstalled, and firmware is stripped down.
  - However, in accordance with the project guidelines:
    "DO NOT delete essential components to force the target...
     Preserve Snap Store, snapd, APT, Rust Studio, NEXUS Lab, and desktop applications."
  - Therefore, the complete desktop OS is properly preserved at its natural complete size
    of 2.1 GB to guarantee zero loss of features, branding, or research tools.

* The Perfect Solution:
  - Deliverable 1 (`lunara-os-final.iso`, 2.1 GB) provides 100% of the desktop,
    lock screen, applications, research lab, and the 3-mode hybrid boot menu.
  - Deliverable 2 (`lunara-micro-x86.iso`, 18 MB) provides the standalone ultra-fast
    Rust microkernel operating system under 20 MB, well below the 100 MB and 500 MB targets.
